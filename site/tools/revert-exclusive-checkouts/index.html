Revert Exclusive Checkouts

Files with the exclusive checkout modifier (+l) can only be checked out by one person at a time. When someone leaves a file checked out and isn't around to release it, revert their checkout here so the rest of the team can get back to work.

Where to look

Exclusive checkouts

File Checked out by Workspace Change

Their local copies aren't touched, but any changes they made to these files stop being tracked by Perforce. Let them know before they lose work.
